Single-key shortcuts are ignored while you're typing in a text field, so they never interfere with editing. The Ctrl combinations always work.

Full keyboard flow: ↑↓ to find a caption, E to edit it, Ctrl+↑↓ to keep editing down the list, Esc when done.

Upload to Criblate

  1. One-time setup: drag this button to your bookmarks bar ⇪ DubDesk → Criblate
  2. Click Open Criblate below. Your captions are sent to that tab and also copied to the clipboard.
  3. When the transcript page has loaded (log in first if asked), click the DubDesk → Criblate bookmark. It fills every caption box in order and saves the draft.
