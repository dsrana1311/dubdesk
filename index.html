Recording settings

Microphone

Leave these off for clean, natural takes — they’re made for calls and smear a performance. Turn them on only for a laptop mic in a noisy room.

Use headphones, or the speakers will feed back. Adds a little delay; an interface’s direct monitoring is better if you have one.

Cueing

The classic ADR cue: beep, beep, beep — and speak on the silent fourth. Needs a pre-roll of 3 seconds for all three.

Recording pace

Below 1× the video plays slower while you record, then the take is sped back up to 1× without changing the pitch of your voice.

Prompter

The line scrolls past a sync bar: say each word as it crosses. You see every word coming, so there’s no reaction delay.
